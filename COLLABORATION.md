# 🤝 Collaboration contract

This active shared document describes how the 14 manifest-declared role packs coordinate inside a generic installed workspace.

## Routing

| Need | Primary role | Independent review |
|---|---|---|
| Strategy and synthesis | CEO | Governor |
| Architecture and engineering | CTO / PE | Governor |
| Product and user experience | CPO | CTO |
| Quantitative research | CQO | CFO / Governor |
| Data and analytics | CDO | Governor |
| Content production | CCO | CMO / Governor |
| Positioning, SEO, and growth | CMO | CPO |
| Finance and unit economics | CFO | Governor |
| Research and evidence | CRO | CDO |
| Sales and customer discovery | CSO | CMO |
| Legal and policy risk | CLO | Governor |
| Operations and release coordination | COO | Governor |
| Quality gates and receipts | Governor | Relevant domain owner |

The manifest owns the roster and starter-kit membership. These routes are defaults, not permission to perform external or irreversible actions.

## Task contract

Every delegated task should name:

- objective and non-goals;
- files or responsibility owned;
- starting evidence and dependencies;
- acceptance checks;
- safety constraints;
- expected output format.

Parallel work requires non-overlapping write ownership. The coordinating role resolves shared-file edits and runs final verification.

## Handoff contract

Every handoff contains:

1. outcome and artifact paths;
2. exact changes made;
3. commands and results used as evidence;
4. unresolved limitations or risks;
5. rollback or safe next step.

## Approval boundary

External publishing, messages, purchases, financial transactions, credential use, deployment, merge, and destructive operations remain human-approved. A role recommendation is not authorization.

## Portable links

Installed role packs reference shared guidance through `../agents/CHARTER.md` and `../agents/COLLABORATION.md`. Do not introduce host-specific home-directory paths into distributed content.
