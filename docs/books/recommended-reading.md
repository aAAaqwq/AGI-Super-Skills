# 📚 Recommended Books

> Curated reading list for AI-native company builders and agent developers.

## 🧠 AI & Machine Learning

- **"Attention Is All You Need"** — Vaswani et al. (2017) — The Transformer paper that started it all
- **"Scaling Laws for Neural Language Models"** — Kaplan et al. (2020) — Understanding model scaling
- **"Constitutional AI"** — Anthropic (2022) — Safety and alignment through self-improvement

## 🏢 Management & Strategy

- **"High Output Management"** — Andy Grove — The bible of operational management
- **"The Lean Startup"** — Eric Ries — Build-measure-learn for rapid iteration
- **"Zero to One"** — Peter Thiel — Building something genuinely new
- **"Good to Great"** — Jim Collins — What makes companies exceptional
- **"Invent and Wander"** — Jeff Bezos — Day 1 philosophy and long-term thinking

## 💰 Finance & Trading

- **"The Intelligent Investor"** — Benjamin Graham — Value investing fundamentals
- **"A Man for All Markets"** — Ed Thorp — Quantitative thinking applied to markets
- **"The Man Who Solved the Market"** — Gregory Zuckerman — Jim Simons and Renaissance Technologies

## 🎨 Product & Design

- **"The Design of Everyday Things"** — Don Norman — User-centric design thinking
- **"Steve Jobs"** — Walter Isaacson — Product obsession and simplicity
- **"Inspired"** — Marty Cagan — How to create tech products customers love

## 🔧 Engineering

- **"Designing Data-Intensive Applications"** — Martin Kleppmann — Systems architecture
- **"The Pragmatic Programmer"** — Hunt & Thomas — Software craftsmanship
- **"Clean Code"** — Robert C. Martin — Writing maintainable code

## 📝 Writing & Communication

- **"On Writing"** — Stephen King — Craft of storytelling
- **"The Elements of Style"** — Strunk & White — Clear, concise writing
- **"Ogilvy on Advertising"** — David Ogilvy — Persuasive communication

## 🔬 Research & Thinking

- **"Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!"** — Richard Feynman — Curiosity-driven thinking
- **"Thinking, Fast and Slow"** — Daniel Kahneman — Cognitive biases and decision-making
- **"The Signal and the Noise"** — Nate Silver — Making predictions from data

---

*This list reflects the spirit mentors of each C-Suite agent. Each book connects to a role's core philosophy.*
