This calendar begins only after G0–G4. Dates are assigned by the human owner; empty slots are intentional.

| Week | Artifact | Channel | Owner action | Required evidence | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | MVTA walkthrough | Repository only | Validate and invite private review | Green checks, pinned revision, recovery | Revise/ready |
| 2 | Safe-preview build note | X or one approved community | Edit and post manually | Claim ledger, link check, disclosure | Continue/stop |
| 3 | No planned post | Feedback review | Summarize questions and corrections | Sanitized feedback log | Fix/hold |
| 4 | Distribution comparison | One best-fit channel | Post manually if materially useful | Harness matrix verified | Continue/stop |
| 5 | No planned post | Documentation work | Improve repeated setup friction | Reproduction evidence | Fix/hold |
| 6 | Starter-kit case study | One best-fit channel | Post manually | No external action; limitations stated | Continue/stop |
| 7–8 | No default campaign | Measurement review | Complete G6 | Metric quality and harm review | Deepen/end |

Repeat only with a new artifact or material evidence. Never fill the calendar with duplicate posts to maintain cadence.

## Per-item approval

Record owner, reviewer, intended audience, source revision, claim-ledger row, scheduled time, platform-rule check, alt text, manual post confirmation, and correction/removal decision.
