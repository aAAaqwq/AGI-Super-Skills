# Go To Market runbook

This runbook produces a claim-safe launch and revenue experiment. It does not automatically message prospects, publish content, buy media, or change customer systems. Outcome validation is **Validation pending** until a matching receipt exists.

## Input

Provide the validated problem, product evidence, target segment, positioning assumptions, price boundaries, launch horizon, approved channels, budget, and prohibited claims. The core is `ceo`, `cpo`, `cmo`, `cco`, `cso`, and `governor`; use `cro`, `cfo`, `clo`, and `cdo` when their evidence is material.

## Waves

1. `ceo` defines the launch decision, owners, budget ceiling, and manual gates.
2. `cro` and `cpo` validate audience evidence and product promises.
3. `cmo` owns positioning and experiment design; `cco` owns sourced creative; `cso` owns discovery and revenue handoff.
4. `cfo` checks unit economics, `clo` checks terms and claim risk, and `cdo` checks measurement semantics.
5. `governor` challenges the combined evidence before CEO synthesis.

Keep shared launch claims under one owner. Only independent research, economics, legal, and measurement reviews may run concurrently.

## Artifacts

- Positioning brief and audience evidence map.
- Channel-ready launch assets with claim provenance.
- Revenue experiment with qualification, pricing, metric, and stop rule.
- Risk and measurement gate with manual launch checklist.

## Checks

- `audience-evidence-recorded`: segment and problem claims have supporting evidence.
- `claims-and-terms-reviewed`: product, legal, and comparative claims are bounded.
- `unit-economics-bounded`: spend, price, and stop conditions are explicit.
- `manual-launch-gate-recorded`: all external actions remain human-triggered.

## Capability fallback

If native delegation is available, cap the active independent lanes to available capacity and keep one owner for shared copy. Otherwise, route manual packets or work sequentially. Missing CRM, analytics, browser, or publishing tools must be reported, not simulated.

## Human approval

A human approves every post, campaign, outreach message, purchase, contract, price change, account connection, and use of customer or prospect data.
