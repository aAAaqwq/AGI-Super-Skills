# Research Decision runbook

This runbook turns a consequential question into a bounded, cited decision rather than an unstructured research dump. Behavioral quality remains **Validation pending** until a matching harness receipt exists.

## Input

Provide the decision to be made, alternatives, affected users, deadline, jurisdiction or financial relevance, known evidence, unavailable evidence, and acceptable uncertainty. The core is `ceo`, `cro`, `cdo`, and `governor`; add `cfo` or `clo` when their domain is material.

## Waves

1. `ceo` frames the decision, alternatives, threshold, and non-goals.
2. `cro` designs answerable questions and retrieves primary evidence; `cdo` checks data meaning, lineage, and analytical validity.
3. `cfo` reviews financial assumptions and `clo` performs legal issue spotting only when triggered by the brief.
4. `governor` challenges source quality, uncertainty, conflicts, and unsupported conclusions.
5. `ceo` records the decision, dissent, expiration conditions, and next evidence needed.

## Artifacts

- Answerable research plan and source strategy.
- Evidence map separating fact, inference, and unknowns.
- Cited synthesis with domain reviews.
- Decision memorandum with expiration and reversal conditions.

## Checks

- `primary-sources-prioritized`: important claims rely on direct authoritative evidence where available.
- `uncertainty-explicit`: conflicts, gaps, and inference are visible.
- `domain-risks-reviewed`: financial or legal conclusions have the proper owner.
- `decision-boundary-recorded`: the memo states what it does and does not authorize.

## Capability fallback

Native delegation may split independent source families or domain reviews. Without it, the CEO issues manual task packets or runs sequential passes. A sequential Governor pass is not independent and must be labeled accordingly.

## Human approval

Research does not authorize purchases, contracts, filings, investments, public claims, or external contact. A human approves any such action after reviewing the final evidence and risks.
