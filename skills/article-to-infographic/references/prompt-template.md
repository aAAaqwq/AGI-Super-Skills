# Prompt 四模块模板

## 模块总览

每个 Prompt 必须按以下顺序组织，缺一不可：

```
[模块1] Main Description       ← 环境、视角、材质
[模块2] Content & Layout        ← 精确的文字位置、排版、层级
[模块3] Context & Environment   ← 真实感环境细节
[模块4] Quality & Style         ← 画质、风格、技术关键词
```

## 模板 A：手写笔记（Hand-written Note）

适用场景：概念定调、金句提炼、个人总结

```
[MAIN DESCRIPTION]
A photorealistic, ultra-clear, ultra-high-resolution close-up photograph of a hand-written study note on a piece of slightly textured cream-white paper. The paper is placed on a warm wooden desk with soft natural window light coming from the upper-left corner, casting gentle shadows. A black gel pen and a red highlighter marker lie to the right side of the paper, uncapped, suggesting active note-taking. The handwriting is dense, legible, and slightly imperfect — showing genuine human handwriting with varying ink pressure.

[CONTENT AND LAYOUT]
All text must be hand-written in black ink with red underlining for emphasis and blue annotations:

Title (top-center, large handwriting, bold): "**[文章核心标题]**"

Subtitle (below title, smaller, red ink): "**[副标题/核心结论]**"

Left column (left 40%):
- Bullet point 1: "**[知识点1]**"
- Bullet point 2: "**[知识点2]**"
- Arrows drawn connecting related points

Right column (right 60%):
- A hand-drawn comparison table with 3 rows × 4 columns
- Column headers in red underline: "**Column1**" | "**Column2**" | "**Column3**" | "**Column4**"
- Row 1: "**Data1**" | "**Data2**" | "**Data3**" | "**Data4**"
- Row 2: "**Data1**" | "**Data2**" | "**Data3**" | "**Data4**"

Bottom section (full width):
- A hand-drawn horizontal line as separator
- Summary conclusion in blue ballpoint: "**[核心结论一句话]**"
- A small hand-drawn star or exclamation mark next to the most important point
- Date written in bottom-right corner: "2026.06.10"

[CONTEXT AND ENVIRONMENT DETAILS]
The paper note is slightly curled at the edges from being held. Small coffee stain ring visible in the upper-right corner, adding authenticity. The surface beneath the paper is a warm mahogany wood desk with visible grain. In the blurred background (shallow depth of field), there's a laptop keyboard and a ceramic coffee mug. The lighting is soft, directional window light creating a calm afternoon study atmosphere.

[QUALITY AND STYLE KEYWORDS]
Hand-written, dense notes, varied ink colors, black gel pen, red highlighter, blue ballpoint annotations, legible text, natural imperfections, warm lighting, shallow depth of field, photorealistic, 8K, ultra-detailed texture, macro photography style, study-vibe, knowledge-dense.
```

Center (central bubble, large bold text, black marker): "**[核心主题]**"

## 通用规则

1. **英文 Prompt 必须**：所有生图 Prompt 用纯英文，中文文字部分用双引号包裹
2. **文字是核心**：每个 Prompt 必须有 30% 以上的篇幅描述文字内容、位置、颜色
3. **真实感优先**：手写的不完美（墨迹不均、纸张纹理、阴影）比完美排版更重要
4. **不要抽象**：避免"beautiful"、"nice"、"good design"等空洞词，用具体描述替代
5. **强制生成文字**：用 `TEXT MUST SAY:` / `READS:` / `bold capital letters spell out:` / `legibly written:` 等指令
